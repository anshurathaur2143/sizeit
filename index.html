Frequently asked questions

Will the image always reach my exact target size?

The tool tries to get the image at or below your target size. Some formats or images may need a reduction in dimensions, and a very small target can reduce quality noticeably.

How do Low, Medium and High quality work?

Low prioritizes smaller files, Medium balances file size and clarity, and High preserves more image detail. Very small target sizes may not be achievable at high quality.

Can I choose a frame size?

Yes. Keep the original image size, choose a square or portrait frame, or enter custom width and height in pixels. Your image is fitted inside the frame without stretching.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Image processing happens directly in your browser, so your selected image is not sent to our servers.

Can I combine multiple images into one PDF?

Yes. Choose up to 20 JPG, PNG or WebP images. Each image becomes a page in a single PDF file that you can download. You can remove individual images before creating the PDF.

Can I convert a PDF into images?

Yes. Upload a PDF to export each page as PNG, JPG or WebP. Each page has its own download button, so you do not need to extract a ZIP file.

About sizeitnow

sizeitnow is a simple, free image utility to help you compress images to a target file size or convert an image into a PDF. Your files are processed in your browser.
