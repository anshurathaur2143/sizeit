Frequently asked questions

Will the image always reach my exact target size?

The tool tries to get the image at or below your target size. Some formats or images may need a reduction in dimensions, and a very small target can reduce quality noticeably.

Are my photos uploaded?

This prototype processes the image in your browser. It does not send the image to a server.

Can I compress PDFs here?

Not in this first prototype. PDF compression can be added as a later feature.
